What counts as one

      
    


    

    
    
      
  1. Chest to fist height. Put a fist on the floor under your chest — touch it, then press. Half a rep is not a rep.
    2. 
      
  2. Arms locked at the top. Every rep starts and finishes straight.
    3. 
      
  3. One line, heels to head. Hips neither sagging nor hitched up. If the line breaks, the set is over.
    4. 
      
  4. Hands under your shoulders, elbows back. Screw your hands outward into the floor so the creases of your elbows face forward and your elbows track back at about 45°, not flared out sideways. That turned-out shoulder is the foundation — it is what lets you press hard without the shoulder complaining.
    5. 
    

    
No bouncing off the floor, and no counting the ones you didn't feel.
